The project

Meet Lake Effect: your personal style guide

Lake Effect started as a way to help friends figure out what to buy for the winter. Now, Lake Effect helps people who don’t have time to shop find clothes that fit their closet and needs, from work outfits to rehearsal dinners.

Meet your guide

Quynh-Nhi is a woman of many hobbies, but her first love has always been fashion. One of her favorite stories from when she was a kid is when she refused to go to school unless her mom let her wear an all-pink outfit. Over time, she has moved away from wearing pink and developed an eye for helping people find clothes and shoes in their personal style. From sneaker recommendations for her coworkers to a workwear shopping trip with her aunt, Quynh-Nhi is always thinking about how a piece might fit into someone’s closet. Styling others has given her an outlet for her love of shopping and her love of connecting with people.
