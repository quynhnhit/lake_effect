Questions

Do I have to buy everything you pick?

Not at all! My recommendations are just suggestions, and we can go through a few rounds if you’re not in love with anything I recommend.

What if I don’t know my personal style?

Totally normal not to know what your style is! To get started, I may ask you to share a few pictures of outfits or pieces you’ve worn and really like, or to build a short mood board. It can also help to try on a few things together, if you have time, to get live feedback on what’s working.

Do you work with any budget?

Yes! I’m happy to work with any budget. If you’re interested, I can recommend secondhand finds from online platforms (like eBay and Poshmark) or go shopping in person at thrift and consignment stores.
